# Privacy Policy

**Drip AI & Data LLC**
**Effective date: August 5, 2026**
**Last updated: August 5, 2026**

Drip AI & Data LLC ("Drip," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

This policy covers:

- **dripaidata.com** — our website, including forms, downloads, and newsletter signups
- **Our Products** — the software applications Drip develops and makes available, including Dynasty War Room and Congressional Trades Fund
- **Client communications** — email, scheduling, and inquiry handling

Consulting engagements are governed by the Master Services Agreement and Statement of Work executed with each client. Where those documents address data handling, they control over this policy for that engagement.

---

## 1. An important note about how our Products work

**Our Products currently run locally.** They are installed on and operated from your own computer or infrastructure. Your data — your configuration, your connected accounts, the content the Product generates, and its history — is stored on your machine, not on servers we control.

This means that for most information processed by our Products, **we are not the party holding it.** You are. We cannot access it, retrieve it, or delete it on your behalf.

Two exceptions apply, and both are described below:

- Information you send us directly, such as support requests or account inquiries
- Information transmitted to third-party AI providers and connected services when the Product runs, which is covered in sections 3 and 4

If we later offer a hosted version of any Product, where we store your data on our infrastructure, that version will be accompanied by supplemental terms describing the additional practices that apply. This policy will be updated before any such version is made available.

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Information you give us

**Website and inquiries.** Name, email address, company name, phone number, and anything you include in a message when you contact us, schedule a call, subscribe to our newsletter, or book a consultation.

**Product licensing and support.** Contact and billing information for the person or organization licensing a Product, and anything you send us when requesting support.

### 2.2 Information we collect automatically on our website

We use PostHog for website analytics. It collects IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring URL, and interaction events. We use this to understand how the site is used and to improve it.

### 2.3 Information our Products handle on your machine

When you run one of our Products, it works with information stored locally on your system. Depending on the Product, this may include:

- Your configuration — brand or company briefs, objectives, target accounts, keywords, filters, thresholds, and schedules
- Access tokens or credentials for services you connect, stored locally
- Data retrieved from services you have connected or directed the Product to monitor
- Content, drafts, recommendations, and research the Product generates
- A history of the Product's output, your edits, and your accept or decline decisions

**We do not receive, store, or have access to any of this.** It remains on your system under your control.

## 3. Artificial intelligence and third-party model providers

Our Products use third-party AI models to analyze information and generate output. Because this involves your data leaving your machine, we want to be specific.

**What is sent.** When the Product runs, it transmits relevant context directly from your system to the AI provider. Depending on the Product, this may include your configuration and brief, content retrieved from services you have connected or directed it to monitor, your recent activity, and your prior edits and decisions.

**Who receives it.**

- **Anthropic PBC** — analysis, reasoning, voice and style modeling, drafting, and research synthesis
- **Google LLC** — image and animated image generation

**This transmission is between you and the provider.** It does not pass through Drip's systems, and we do not receive a copy.

**Provider terms.** We build our Products to use these providers' commercial API tiers, whose terms do not permit the use of submitted content to train their models. Where a Product is configured with your own API credentials, the terms of your account with that provider govern, and you should review them.

**Human review.** Our Products do not publish, send, or take irreversible action automatically. Output is presented to a person for review, and action is taken only when a human acts on it.

We may add or change supported providers as our Products evolve. When we do, we will update this policy.

## 4. Services you connect

Our Products are designed to work with third-party services you authorize — social media platforms, email systems, CRM tools, data platforms, and similar systems.

Credentials for these services are stored locally on your system. When the Product retrieves data from or sends data to a connected service, that exchange is between your system and that service. Drip is not a party to it and does not receive a copy.

Your use of each connected service remains governed by that service's own terms and privacy policy.

## 8. Your choices and rights

**Everyone.** You can access, correct, or delete information we hold about you, opt out of marketing email using the unsubscribe link or by emailing us, and request a copy of your data in a portable format.

For data held by a Product on your own system, deletion is under your control. Our [Data Deletion](https://dripaidata.com/data-deletion) page explains how.

**California residents.** Under the CCPA and CPRA you have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use and disclose it, to request deletion, to request correction, and to be free from discrimination for exercising these rights. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Exercise these rights by emailing danny@dripaidata.com.

**EEA and UK residents.** Under the GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Our legal bases for processing are: performance of a contract, legitimate interests for security and business communications, consent for marketing email, and legal obligation where applicable. Where we transfer data outside the EEA or UK we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or another approved mechanism.

We will respond to rights requests within 30 days, or sooner where required by law.

## 9. Security

For information we hold — website data, inquiries, and business records — we use encrypted transmission, reputable hosted services with their own security programs, and access limited to those who need it.

For information held by a Product on your own system, security is your responsibility. We recommend full-disk encryption, a strong account password, and keeping your operating system current.

No system is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting personal information we hold, we will notify you as required by applicable law.
