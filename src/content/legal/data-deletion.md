# Data Deletion

**Drip AI & Data LLC**
**Last updated: August 5, 2026**

This page explains how to delete data associated with Drip AI & Data products and with our website.

---

## Product data

**Drip products run on your own computer or infrastructure.** Your configuration, connected-account credentials, generated content, and history are stored on your system, not on servers we control. We cannot access this data, and we cannot delete it for you.

Deleting it is straightforward, and entirely under your control.

### Disconnect a single service

To revoke a product's access to one connected service while keeping everything else:

1. Remove the stored credential or access token from the product's configuration
2. Revoke the authorization from the service's own side — most platforms have a connected apps, business integrations, or authorized applications section in their security settings

Revoking from the service side is the more complete step, since it invalidates the token regardless of what remains on your machine.

### Delete all product data

Delete the product's data directory from your system. This removes your configuration, stored credentials, generated content, and history.

If you are unsure where a product stores its data, email **danny@dripaidata.com** and we will tell you the exact path for your installation.

To remove the product entirely, delete the application along with its data directory.

### Content already published

Deleting local data does not remove content you have already published to a third-party platform. Posts, comments, messages, and records created through a product live on that platform. Remove them there.

## Data we hold

We hold a limited amount of information directly: website analytics, inquiries and correspondence, and licensing and billing records.

To request deletion, email **danny@dripaidata.com** with the subject line **"Data Deletion Request"** from the email address associated with your inquiry or account.

**What we delete:**

- Your inquiry and correspondence history
- Your marketing and newsletter subscription
- Website analytics associated with you, where identifiable

**Timing.** Processed within 30 days. Residual copies in backups are purged on a rolling schedule and removed within 90 days.

**What we keep, and why:**

- **Billing and financial records** — retained 7 years as required for tax and accounting. These contain transaction details, not your product data.
- **Anonymized and aggregated statistics** — retained where they cannot identify you.
- **Legal holds** — where we are legally required to preserve information, we retain it for as long as required and delete it afterward.
